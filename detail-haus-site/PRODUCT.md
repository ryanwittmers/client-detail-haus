# Detail Haus

## Product Purpose

Single-page marketing and booking site for Detail Haus, a one-person mobile auto detailing business in Southern Oregon (Medford, Jacksonville, Central Point). Owner Reece runs every job personally. The site's job: communicate credibility and craft, then get the visitor to submit a quote request with vehicle photos.

## Users

People in Southern Oregon who care about their vehicle's condition. They are deliberate buyers — they're not price-shopping, they want to understand quality and trust the operator before they book. Many arrive via referral or word of mouth.

## Brand Tone

Quiet. Precise. Earned. No hype, no adjective overload. The copy reads like the work: nothing extra, everything intentional. Reece does not promise "luxury" — he delivers results you can see.

## Visual Register

Ultra-minimalist dark site. Think editorial car photography, not SaaS dashboard. The palette is near-monochrome: deep ink backgrounds, pure white type, stone gray for secondary content. No color for color's sake. The brand currently uses a burgundy accent that needs to be replaced with a neutral white/off-white treatment — the client wants zero color in the design language.

## Anti-References

- Any "luxury car detailing" site that looks like a car wash franchise
- SaaS landing pages with gradient heroes and hero-metric stat blocks
- Pink, salmon, or warm-red accent colors
- Multiple competing accent colors
- Rounded cards with drop shadows
- Gradient text or rainbow fills
- Stock photography of highways at sunset

## Key Content

- 11 sections: Hero, Services, Pricing, Add-Ons, Process, About, Reviews, Products, Service Area, Instagram, Contact
- Contact form with UploadThing vehicle photo uploads
- Stats: 60 Google reviews, 5.0 rating, 2+ years, 100% owner-operated
- Service area: Medford, Jacksonville, Central Point only
- Packages: The Refresh ($149+), The Full Detail ($299+), The Works ($599+)
